robots.txt

The file

robots.txt is a plain text file at the root of a site. It groups Allow and Disallow path prefixes under User-agent names, and a crawler applies the group that matches its own product token, falling back to the wildcard group addressed to everyone. An empty Disallow means everything is allowed.

This corpus ships a robots.txt that disallows the private directory; the MiniSearch crawler skips those pages, and the crawl log records them as skipped.

Longest prefix wins

When both an Allow and a Disallow prefix match a path, the longer, more specific prefix wins. Ties go to Allow. A Crawl-delay line asks crawlers to wait between requests; MiniSearch honors it by raising its per-host delay to at least the requested value.
