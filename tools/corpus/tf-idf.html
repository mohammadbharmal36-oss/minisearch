TF-IDF Ranking

Term frequency

Term frequency measures how often a query term appears in a document, normalized by the document length so long documents do not win simply by being long. If a term occurs three times in a document of one hundred tokens, its normalized term frequency is low; three occurrences in a document of ten tokens is strong evidence the document is about that term.

Inverse document frequency

Inverse document frequency measures how selective a term is. A term that appears in every document of the collection cannot distinguish anything, so its IDF approaches zero after smoothing. A term that appears in a single document is maximally selective.

MiniSearch uses the smoothed IDF formula log((N + 1) / (df + 1)) + 1, which stays strictly positive and avoids degenerate cases when a term occurs in every document.

Field weighting

Words in a title describe a page more precisely than words buried in a paragraph. MiniSearch therefore weights matches per field: title matches count triple, heading matches count double, and body matches count once. The weights are configurable, and they are applied at ranking time, never at index time.
