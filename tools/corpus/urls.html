URLs and Links

Normalization

Two spellings of the same address should be fetched once. Normalization lowercases the scheme and host, strips the fragment, drops default ports, and keeps the path and query intact. The normalized form is the key in the crawler's visited set.

Relative references

HTML links may be absolute, root-relative, or relative to the current page. Resolving them follows the standard reference resolution algorithm, including dot-segment removal, so a link to ../bm25.html from the ranking page lands on the right document.
