Web Crawlers

The frontier

A crawler starts from seed URLs and follows hyperlinks breadth first. The frontier is the queue of URLs waiting to be fetched; a visited set prevents loops; a depth budget stops the crawl from wandering into the entire web; and a page budget caps the total work per job.

Politeness

A polite crawler respects robots.txt, sends a descriptive User-Agent, and spaces requests to the same host. MiniSearch serializes requests per host with a configurable delay and only parallelizes across different hosts. It also refuses login-looking URLs, because authenticated content is neither public nor polite to scrape.
